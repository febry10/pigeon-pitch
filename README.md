# Pigeon Pitch, One Line

Entry for the make.fun competition "Pigeon Pitch, One Line" (u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf): a one-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup, judged head-to-head on funniness, originality, pigeon voice, and tightness (max 200 characters).

The entry: "Forget drones — fund PeckEx: my flock knows every rooftop, dodges every hawk, and works for crumbs, so we deliver in 10 minutes or poop on your competitor's car for free."

`main.ts` writes that single line deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`) and exits 0.
